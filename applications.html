Applications

Voice-Based Screening for Neurological Health

Neuronous applies voice-based technology to neurological screening. Parkinson's disease is the current validated application, with potential to support broader neurological research in the future.

Start Screening Future Directions

Current Validated Application

Parkinson's Disease Screening

Parkinson's disease is the first application of our voice-based technology to have been validated. Voice carries measurable characteristics that may be relevant to neurological assessment. Our system analyzes these voice-derived signals computationally to support screening.

Screening supports early awareness and clinical conversation. It does not provide a diagnosis.

Status Validated application
Focus Parkinson's disease screening
Input Voice-derived signal
Output Screening support

Why Voice

Voice as a measurable signal

Speech and voice contain acoustic and temporal characteristics that researchers can study in relation to neurological function. Changes in these characteristics may carry information relevant to screening — which is what our approach is designed to analyze.

The technology captures voice information, analyzes measurable voice characteristics using computational methods, and produces a screening output — it is not a diagnostic system.
