WHY VOICE-BASED ASSESSMENT?

Benefits of Using Voice Signals in Health Assessment

  • 01

    Non-Invasive

    Voice collection does not require physical intervention.

  • 02

    Remote

    Voice can be collected remotely.

  • 03

    Cost-Effective

    Voice-based assessment can support a cost-effective approach.

  • 04

    Accessible

    Voice-based assessment can be accessed through digital devices.

  • 05

    Repeatable

    Voice can be recorded repeatedly over time.

  • 06

    Longitudinal

    Repeated assessments can support observation of patterns over time.
