Privacy

Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy is central to how Neuronous approaches voice-based screening. This page explains — in plain terms — how we think about data, what is involved in the screening process, and the principles that guide our work.

This page describes principles and structure, not a finalized legal policy. Specific operational details will be published as they are confirmed.

Introduction

Why privacy matters to Neuronous

Screening technologies work with information that is personal. Because our approach is built on voice, we take the responsible handling of that information as a starting condition for the technology — not as an afterthought.

Voice is one of the most personal signals a person can share. It carries identity, expression and context. Any screening technology that works with voice therefore has an obligation to treat that data with care, and to be transparent about what it does with it.

This page describes the principles that guide our approach to privacy and data protection. It is written to be readable rather than legalistic, and it reflects our current understanding of the responsibilities involved in building a voice-based screening technology.

Voice Data

Voice and audio in the screening process

The screening process is built around voice. Understanding what that means — and what role voice data plays — is part of understanding how the technology works.

01

What voice data is

Voice data refers to recordings or derived representations of a person's voice that are captured during the screening process. These signals are the input to the analysis step.

02

Its role in analysis

The voice signal is processed computationally so that measurable characteristics can be analyzed. The result of that analysis contributes to a screening output — it is not a medical diagnosis.

03

Why it is sensitive

Voice can carry information beyond the words being spoken. For this reason, voice data is treated as sensitive information and handled with the same care as other personal data.

Important Distinction

Screening Is Not Diagnosis

Neuronous provides a screening technology. A screening result is not a medical diagnosis, and it does not replace evaluation by a qualified clinician. Any medical decision should be made in consultation with a healthcare professional.
