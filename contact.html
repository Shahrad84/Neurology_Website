FAQ

Common questions

A short set of questions we are frequently asked. For privacy-related details, please refer to the Privacy page.

  • How does the screening work?

    A voice sample is recorded and analyzed computationally to identify patterns relevant to neurological screening. The output is a screening result — not a medical diagnosis.

  • How long does the screening take?

    The screening process is designed to be quick and non-invasive. Specific timing information will be provided once the screening platform is available.

  • How do I receive my result?

    A screening output is produced as part of the process. Details on how the result is delivered will be provided together with the screening platform.

  • How is my voice data used?

    Voice data is used to perform the analysis that supports screening. More detail is available in our Privacy page. Learn more in Privacy

  • Is the screening a diagnosis?

    No. The screening result is not a substitute for a medical diagnosis or professional medical advice. Any medical decision should be made in consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
