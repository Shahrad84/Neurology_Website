Science & Collaboration

Built at the intersection of disciplines

Developing meaningful screening technology requires more than one kind of expertise. It brings together people working on the technical side of signal and voice analysis with those who understand neurological research and clinical practice. Progress depends on these perspectives being connected — not treated separately.
