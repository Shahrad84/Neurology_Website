Neuro intelligence for better health

Voice-based screening for neurological disorders

Neuronous develops voice-based technology for neurological screening. A short voice sample is analyzed computationally to identify patterns relevant to screening — designed to support early awareness, not to provide a diagnosis.

Start Screening Contact Us

Screening supports awareness and early conversation — it does not replace a clinical diagnosis.

The Problem

Accessible neurological screening matters

Earlier identification of potential neurological indicators can support timely follow-up. Yet neurological assessment often depends on specialized clinical settings and expertise that may not always be easily accessible.

Early screening

Access challenges

Why voice

Voice contains measurable acoustic and temporal characteristics that can be computationally analyzed and studied in relation to neurological conditions.
