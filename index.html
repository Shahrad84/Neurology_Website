Voice-Based Screening Technology

Voice-Based Screening for Neurological Disorders

A non-invasive technology that analyzes the voice as a signal — designed for early screening, not diagnosis. Parkinson's disease is our first validated application.

Explore Technology Contact Us

Screening supports awareness and early conversation — it does not replace a clinical diagnosis.

The Idea

What if your voice could provide a measurable signal?

Small variations in voice — pitch, rhythm, timing, stability — can carry information about neurological function. We turn those variations into a structured, quantitative signal.

Our First Validated Application

Parkinson's Disease

Parkinson's disease is the first application where our voice-based screening approach has been validated. The technology is designed to flag vocal patterns associated with the condition — supporting earlier awareness and clinical conversation, not replacing a diagnosis.
