What is Neuronous?

Neuronous is an AI-powered platform that analyzes voice signals to detect meaningful patterns related to neurological and overall health. It turns subtle voice changes into actionable insights for earlier detection, better monitoring and more informed care.

Why voice?

Voice reflects a unique combination of neurological, physical and cognitive functions. It provides a non-invasive, convenient and cost-effective way to monitor health and detect changes early.
