The Technology

Turning Voice Into a Signal for Neurological Screening

Our technology analyzes acoustic and temporal characteristics of the voice using computational methods, identifying patterns that may be relevant to neurological screening. It is designed to support screening — not to provide a diagnosis.

See How It Works Contact Us

Why Voice

Voice carries measurable information

Speech and voice contain acoustic and temporal characteristics — such as pitch, rhythm and stability over time — that can be described computationally. These characteristics are the raw material our technology is designed to analyze.

Process

How it works

Four steps carry the voice from capture to screening output — each one prepares the signal for the next.

  1. 01

    Voice Capture

    The user provides a voice sample through a supported interface.

  2. 02

    Signal Processing

    The captured voice is prepared and represented as a signal suitable for analysis.

  3. 03

    Computational Analysis

    Relevant patterns in the signal are evaluated using our computational models.

  4. 04

    Screening Output

    The system produces an output intended to support neurological screening — not a diagnosis.

Important Distinction

Screening Is Not Diagnosis

This technology is intended to support neurological screening. It does not replace professional medical evaluation, clinical assessment or a definitive diagnosis. A screening result is a signal for further conversation — not a conclusion.
