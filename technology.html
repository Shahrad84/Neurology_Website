The Technology

Turning Voice Into a Signal for Neurological Screening

Neuronous analyzes acoustic and temporal characteristics of the voice using computational methods to identify patterns that may be relevant to neurological screening. It is designed to support screening — not to provide a diagnosis.

Start Screening See How It Works

Why Voice

Voice carries measurable information

Speech and voice contain acoustic and temporal characteristics — such as pitch, rhythm and stability over time — that can be described computationally. Neurological changes can influence these characteristics, which is why voice is being studied as a measurable signal for screening.

Process

How it works

Five steps carry the voice from capture to screening output — signal processing, feature extraction, and AI-assisted analysis each prepare the information for the next stage.

  1. 01

    Voice Capture

    The user provides a voice sample through a supported screening interface.

  2. 02

    Signal Processing

    The captured voice is prepared and represented as a signal suitable for analysis.

  3. 03

    Feature Extraction

    Measurable characteristics of the voice and speech signal are extracted for analysis.

  4. 04

    AI / Computational Analysis

    Computational methods analyze the extracted features and identify patterns that may be relevant to neurological screening.

  5. 05

    Screening Output

    The system produces a screening output intended to support neurological screening — not a diagnosis.

Important Distinction

Screening Is Not Diagnosis

This technology is intended to support neurological screening. It does not replace professional medical evaluation, clinical assessment or a definitive diagnosis. A screening result is a signal for further conversation — not a conclusion.
