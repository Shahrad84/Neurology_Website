Science & Evidence

Research behind the technology

Neuronous is grounded in research on voice signals and their relevance to neurological screening. This page documents the scientific foundation and validation approach behind the technology.

Explore the Technology Publications & References

Research Overview

Voice as a measurable research signal

Voice and speech contain measurable acoustic and temporal characteristics. Research investigates whether these characteristics carry information relevant to neurological function — the foundation of our screening approach.

The Technology page explains how the underlying system works. This page documents the scientific foundation and evidence behind it.

Important Distinction

Screening Is Not Diagnosis

The scientific evidence behind a screening approach does not turn that approach into a diagnosis. This technology is intended to support neurological screening and does not replace professional medical evaluation, clinical assessment or a definitive diagnosis.
